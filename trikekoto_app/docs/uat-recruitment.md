# UAT recruitment — messages

Drafts for finding the ten participants step 78 needs: five TODA drivers from
the chapter you intend to pilot with, and five people who actually ride
tricycles.

> **These are drafts by a non-native speaker.** Put them on the same review
> sheet as the in-app copy (`filipino-copy-review.md`) before sending. The
> failure mode here is worse than in the app: awkward wording in a screen is
> a usability finding, awkward wording in a first approach to a chapter
> president is a closed door.

---

## 0 · First contact — Messenger or SMS

Almost nobody's first contact with a chapter officer is a letter. This is the
message that gets a meeting; section 1 is what you say **at** the meeting.

Short on purpose. A long first message from a stranger reads as a pitch, and
the only thing it has to do is get a yes to a conversation.

> Magandang po. Si Jelo po ito, estudyante sa PRMSU–San Marcelino.
>
> May ginawa po akong app para sa pagbo-book ng tricycle bilang thesis ko, at
> gusto ko po sanang ipakita sa inyo — kayo po muna, bago ang kahit sinong
> driver.
>
> Puwede po ba kayong makausap ng mga **15 minuto** sa terminal, kahit anong
> araw na libre kayo? Wala pong bayad at walang obligasyon — gusto ko lang
> pong ipakita at tanungin kung puwedeng subukan ng ilang driver ninyo.
>
> Salamat po!

If there is no reply in three days, send once more — officers are busy and a
message is easy to miss — and then stop. A third message is pressure, and the
person you are pressuring is the one whose cooperation the whole pilot needs.

> Magandang po, follow-up ko lang po sa message ko noong [araw]. Kung hindi po
> kayo available ngayon, puwede po akong maghintay — sabihin ninyo lang po
> kung kailan.

## 1 · The chapter officer — ask first

Send or say this **before** approaching any individual driver. A student who
recruits a chapter's members without asking the chapter has made an enemy of
the one person whose cooperation the pilot depends on.

> Magandang araw po, [Ginoo/Ginang ______].
>
> Ako po si Jelo Jian Sabroso, estudyante sa PRMSU–San Marcelino. Ang thesis
> ko po ay isang app para sa pagbo-book ng tricycle — **TrikeKoTo** — at
> gusto ko pong subukan ito kasama ang inyong chapter.
>
> Ang hihilingin ko lang po sa ngayon ay **mga 45 minuto kasama ang lima
> ninyong driver**, isa-isa. Susubukan lang po nila ang app sa sarili nilang
> telepono at sasabihin kung ano ang nakikita nila. Walang bayad, walang
> obligasyon, at puwede pong huminto kahit kailan.
>
> Kayo po muna ang gusto kong tanungin bago ko po lapitan ang kahit sinong
> driver. Kung may gusto po kayong makita muna — ang app, ang mga tanong, o
> ang pahintulot na papipirmahan ko — ipapakita ko po nang buo.
>
> Salamat po,
> Jelo

**Bring to that conversation:** the app on your own phone, ready to
demonstrate; a printed consent form; and a straight answer to *"ano ang
mapapala namin dito?"* — which is a fair question and deserves better than
"it's for my thesis".

Here is one, and it is the honest version. Say it in your own words:

> Sa ngayon po, wala pong bayad at walang kita — pagsubok pa lang po ito.
> Ang mapapala po ninyo ngayon: makikita ninyo kung paano gumagana, at ang
> sasabihin ng mga driver ninyo ang magbabago sa app bago ito gamitin ng
> kahit sino. Kung sa tingin ninyo po ay hindi ito nakakatulong sa chapter,
> iyon po ay sagot din — at iyon ang itatala ko.
>
> Kung sakali pong gusto ninyong subukan nang totohanan pagkatapos, kayo po
> ang magdedesisyon, hindi ako. Libre po ang app para sa chapter; ang gastos
> po ay nasa akin.

**What not to say, however much you want to:** that it will bring them more
passengers, that the LGU or the TODA federation is interested, or that it will
be adopted. None of those are yours to promise, and a chapter that agreed on
that basis has been misled rather than recruited.

## 2 · A driver — after the chapter agrees

Short, and said in person where possible. A message asking someone to install
software reads like a scam; the same words from a person standing there do not.

> Kuya/Ate, estudyante po ako sa PRMSU. May ginawa po akong app para sa
> pagbo-book ng tricycle, at pinayagan na po ako ni [chapter officer] na
> subukan ito kasama ang ilang driver dito.
>
> Mga **45 minuto po**. Ilalagay ko po ito sa telepono ninyo, susubukan
> ninyo, at sasabihin ninyo kung ano ang nakakalito. **Ang app po ang
> sinusubukan, hindi kayo** — kung may hindi kayo maintindihan, ako po ang
> may mali, hindi kayo.
>
> Hindi po kailangan ang inyong email o plate number — bibigyan ko po kayo
> ng test na detalye. Buburahin po ang test account pagkatapos, at ang app
> kung gusto ninyo.

**Do not promise income, passengers, or that the chapter will adopt it.** You
do not control any of those, and a driver who agreed on that basis will feel
misled when the pilot ends.

## 3 · A commuter

> Hi po! Estudyante po ako sa PRMSU. May app po akong ginawa para sa
> pagbo-book ng tricycle, at naghahanap po ako ng mga sumasakay ng tricycle
> na puwedeng sumubok.
>
> Mga **30–45 minuto** po, sa telepono ninyo mismo. Wala pong tama o
> mali — kung may nakalito, iyon mismo ang kailangan kong malaman.
>
> Wala pong bayad, pero malaking tulong po sa thesis ko.

**Deliberately include one person who is not confident with smartphones.** The
kit says that participant will teach you more than the other four combined,
and it is right — but they are also the one least likely to volunteer, so ask
them directly rather than posting and hoping.

---

## What to say when they ask

**"Magkano ang bayad?"** — Wala. Say it plainly and early; a vague answer
makes people assume there is money and feel cheated later.

**"Kailangan ba ang totoong ID ko?"** — Hindi. You hand them a dummy card.
Say this *before* the session, not when the screen appears — someone who
arrives worried about their licence has already had a worse session.

**"Ano ang gagawin ninyo sa number ko?"** — *Commuters:* the app sends one SMS
code to it, and you delete the account together the same day; the number is
also removed from their test rides. *Drivers* never give one — they register
with the test details you hand them. The test account is removed from the
admin panel the same day, but the finished test rides stay as the chapter's
record, still carrying the driver's name, plate and email.

**"Gagamitin ba ito ng TODA namin?"** — You do not know. Say so. The pilot
plan is step 89 and it is the chapter's decision, not yours.

## 4 · After the chapter agrees — settling the day

Send this once, to the officer, when you have a date. It makes the logistics
somebody else's expectation rather than your hope.

> Salamat po ulit. Narito po ang mga detalye:
>
> - **Petsa at oras:** [araw], mga [oras] — mga 45 minuto po bawat driver,
>   isa-isa lang po.
> - **Saan:** sa terminal po mismo, kung puwede.
> - **Ilan:** lima pong driver. Kung may mas gusto kayong isama o hindi
>   isama, kayo po ang bahala.
> - **Dala ko po:** ang app, pekeng ID card para sa pagsubok, at ang
>   pahintulot na papipirmahan.
> - **Dala nila:** ang sarili nilang telepono, at data kung meron — ako po
>   ang bahala kung wala.
>
> Kung may magbago po sa schedule ninyo, sabihin ninyo lang po — madali po
> akong makibagay.

And afterwards, the same day, to each participant and to the officer:

> Salamat po talaga sa oras ninyo. Nabura ko na po ang test account na
> ginamit natin. Kung may naisip pa po kayong dapat ayusin sa app, sabihin
> ninyo lang po — iyon po ang pinakamahalagang bahagi nito.

**Say the deletion out loud, and mean it.** The consent promised it; a
participant who hears it confirmed is the one who tells the next driver this
was worth doing.

---

## Practical

- **Ten participants, five per role.** Recruit twelve; two will not turn up.
- **Their handset, their mobile data.** Yours is fast, charged, and already
  has permissions granted. Theirs is the real test.
- **Send the install link ahead** where you can:
  `https://trikekoto.web.app/download/trikekoto.apk` — a participant who
  arrives with it installed gives you a session about the system rather than
  about sideloading.
- **Record handset model and Android version** for each person.
- **One session at a time.** Two drivers watching each other produces one
  opinion, not two.
