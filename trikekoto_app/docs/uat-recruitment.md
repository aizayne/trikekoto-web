# UAT recruitment — messages

Drafts for finding the ten participants step 78 needs: five TODA drivers from
the chapter you intend to pilot with, and five people who actually ride
tricycles.

> **These are drafts by a non-native speaker.** Put them on the same review
> sheet as the in-app copy (`filipino-copy-review.md`) before sending. The
> failure mode here is worse than in the app: awkward wording in a screen is
> a usability finding, awkward wording in a first approach to a chapter
> president is a closed door.

---

## 1 · The chapter officer — ask first

Send or say this **before** approaching any individual driver. A student who
recruits a chapter's members without asking the chapter has made an enemy of
the one person whose cooperation the pilot depends on.

> Magandang araw po, [Ginoo/Ginang ______].
>
> Ako po si Jelo Jian Sabroso, estudyante sa PRMSU–San Marcelino. Ang thesis
> ko po ay isang app para sa pagbo-book ng tricycle — **TrikeKoTo** — at
> gusto ko pong subukan ito kasama ang inyong chapter.
>
> Ang hihilingin ko lang po sa ngayon ay **mga 30 minuto kasama ang lima
> ninyong driver**, isa-isa. Susubukan lang po nila ang app sa sarili nilang
> telepono at sasabihin kung ano ang nakikita nila. Walang bayad, walang
> obligasyon, at puwede pong huminto kahit kailan.
>
> Kayo po muna ang gusto kong tanungin bago ko po lapitan ang kahit sinong
> driver. Kung may gusto po kayong makita muna — ang app, ang mga tanong, o
> ang pahintulot na papipirmahan ko — ipapakita ko po nang buo.
>
> Salamat po,
> Jelo

**Bring to that conversation:** the app on your own phone, ready to
demonstrate; a printed consent form; and a straight answer to *"ano ang
mapapala namin dito?"* — which is a fair question and deserves better than
"it's for my thesis".

---

## 2 · A driver — after the chapter agrees

Short, and said in person where possible. A message asking someone to install
software reads like a scam; the same words from a person standing there do not.

> Kuya/Ate, estudyante po ako sa PRMSU. May ginawa po akong app para sa
> pagbo-book ng tricycle, at pinayagan na po ako ni [chapter officer] na
> subukan ito kasama ang ilang driver dito.
>
> Mga **30 minuto lang po**. Ilalagay ko po ito sa telepono ninyo, susubukan
> ninyo, at sasabihin ninyo kung ano ang nakakalito. **Ang app po ang
> sinusubukan, hindi kayo** — kung may hindi kayo maintindihan, ako po ang
> may mali, hindi kayo.
>
> Buburahin ko rin po lahat pagkatapos, pati ang app kung gusto ninyo.

**Do not promise income, passengers, or that the chapter will adopt it.** You
do not control any of those, and a driver who agreed on that basis will feel
misled when the pilot ends.

## 3 · A commuter

> Hi po! Estudyante po ako sa PRMSU. May app po akong ginawa para sa
> pagbo-book ng tricycle, at naghahanap po ako ng mga sumasakay ng tricycle
> na puwedeng sumubok.
>
> Mga **20–30 minuto** lang po, sa telepono ninyo mismo. Wala pong tama o
> mali — kung may nakalito, iyon mismo ang kailangan kong malaman.
>
> Wala pong bayad, pero malaking tulong po sa thesis ko.

**Deliberately include one person who is not confident with smartphones.** The
kit says that participant will teach you more than the other four combined,
and it is right — but they are also the one least likely to volunteer, so ask
them directly rather than posting and hoping.

---

## What to say when they ask

**"Magkano ang bayad?"** — Wala. Say it plainly and early; a vague answer
makes people assume there is money and feel cheated later.

**"Kailangan ba ang totoong ID ko?"** — Hindi. You hand them a dummy card.
Say this *before* the session, not when the screen appears — someone who
arrives worried about their licence has already had a worse session.

**"Ano ang gagawin ninyo sa number ko?"** — The app sends one SMS code, and
you delete the whole account the same day, in front of them.

**"Gagamitin ba ito ng TODA namin?"** — You do not know. Say so. The pilot
plan is step 89 and it is the chapter's decision, not yours.

---

## Practical

- **Ten participants, five per role.** Recruit twelve; two will not turn up.
- **Their handset, their mobile data.** Yours is fast, charged, and already
  has permissions granted. Theirs is the real test.
- **Send the install link ahead** where you can:
  `https://trikekoto.web.app/download/trikekoto.apk` — a participant who
  arrives with it installed gives you a session about the system rather than
  about sideloading.
- **Record handset model and Android version** for each person.
- **One session at a time.** Two drivers watching each other produces one
  opinion, not two.
